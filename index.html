Australian literary awards · 2024–2026

Behind Australia's literary shortlists

Literary awards bring a small selection of books into view. Which publishers appear most often, and do they appear across many categories or focus on a few? We begin with the Prime Minister’s Literary Awards.

01 / The award landscape

Prime Minister’s Literary Awards

The 2024–2026 Prime Minister’s Literary Awards data contains 90 shortlisted or winning books across six categories. These charts examine which publishers are represented in that selection.

Which publishers appear the most often?

Allen & Unwin leads the list

Its 18 appearances are more than twice the next publisher’s eight.

Together, these ten publishers account for 65 of the 90 listed books. The chart measures visibility in this award selection, not publishers’ overall output.

Equal totals can hide different patterns

03 / From lists to winners

Does visibility lead to wins?

Appearing often can increase a publisher’s presence on an award list. The winning books tell a more specific story.

Do the most visible publishers win more often?

Listed books and winners by publisher · Prime Minister’s Literary Awards, 2024–2026

The same number of wins, with fewer appearances

University of Queensland Press and Allen & Unwin each have three wins, from eight and 18 listed books respectively.

Each dot is a publisher with at least three listed books. The dashed line marks one win per five appearances, the overall ratio across these awards.

Two publishers with three appearances and one win are nudged slightly apart so both dots can be seen. Hover for exact counts. A publisher’s small set of appearances should not be treated as a reliable win rate.
