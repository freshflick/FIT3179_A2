Stella’s longlists broaden beyond fiction

04 / The geography of literary visibility

Publishing hubs in a wider reading landscape

The ten leading publishers map to four documented office metro areas. A separate historical library snapshot shows how much more widely reading spaces are located.

Most appearances cluster in Sydney and Melbourne

Office metro areas of the ten most represented publishers · Prime Minister’s Literary Awards, 2024–2026

Sydney accounts for the largest share

Publishers mapped to Sydney account for 37 of the 65 books from the top ten publishers. Melbourne follows with 17; Brisbane and Broome contribute eight and three.

The story in brief

Visibility clusters; recognition varies.

Ten publishers account for 65 of 90 PMLA books. Across PMLA and Stella, only nine of 117 distinct titles appear on both lists. The mapped publisher offices cluster in four metros; historical library sites offer a wider geographic context.

Sources and method

Book lists: Creative Australia and the Stella Prize, 2024–2026. These lists represent selected books, not all Australian publishing.

Map geography: ABS boundaries, publisher office sources, and RMIT historical library locations. Chart specifications and prepared data are in the JSON folder.
